Project Summary & Game Mechanics OverviewThis project is a 2D side-view strategy defense game inspired by Plants vs. Zombies. The primary goal is to defend a Home Base (located on the far-left edge of the screen) from three escalating waves of incoming enemies advancing horizontally from the right.   Key Game Systems & LogicAnimated Cutscene (10–20 seconds): Displays keyframe character animations, narrative dialogue/subtitles, and conflict setup before gameplay launches.   Playfield Grid Layout: Features at least 3 horizontal lanes (standardized to 4 lanes in our design) with a distinct Home Base on the left and enemy spawns on the right.   Player Unit Advancement: Placed player units (except stationary Defense units) automatically march left-to-right along their assigned lane, engaging encountered enemies.   Player Unit Typology:Melee Player: Costs 10 coins, 5 HP, close-range attack (1 damage/hit), starter unit.   Ranged Player: Costs 20 coins, 5 HP, fires horizontal projectiles (1 damage/hit), unlocks at 20 coins.   Defense Player: Costs 30 coins, 7 HP, stationary blocker, shields trailing friendly units from horizontal attacks, unlocks at 30 coins.   Enemy Unit Typology:Easy (Upper Ranged): 5 HP, normal speed, fires high projectiles (duck to dodge), 1 damage/hit.   Medium (Lower Ranged): 5 HP, normal speed, fires low projectiles (jump to dodge), 1 damage/hit.   Hard (Melee): 7 HP, fast runner speed, close-range attack (2 damage/hit).   Evasive Key Controls:Left-clicking highlights and locks lane selection.   Pressing W / Up Arrow causes active players in selected lanes to jump over low attacks.   Pressing S / Down Arrow causes active players in selected lanes to duck under high attacks.   Defense units remain stationary and are unaffected by jump/duck commands.   Economy & UI Toolbar:Starts with 30 coins.   Defeated enemies drop collectible coins gathered upon player collision.   UI cards display in grayscale when unaffordable and transition to full color when affordable.   Multi-Wave Spawner:Wave 1: $\ge 3$ Easy enemies.   Wave 2: $\ge 5$ Easy/Medium enemies.   Wave 3: $\ge 10$ Easy/Medium/Hard enemies.   Win/Lose Conditions:Win: Defeat all enemies across all 3 waves.   Lose: Any enemy breaches the Home Base boundary on the left, triggering an instant scene freeze and Lose Menu.   Visual & Scenery Standards:Cohesive custom theme (e.g., Sci-Fi Lab, Fantasy Castle, Beach).   At least 5 distinct environmental decor assets.   Distinct composite geometric character models (no recoloring alone).   Red fragment color tinting or downscaling visual feedback upon taking damage.   4.
